A Hyderabad-based cardiologist, Dr. Mukharjee Madivada,
 recently recounted a critical case that underscored the importance of timely medical intervention
 and the dangers of uninformed advice. His story involved a 39-year-old patient who
  exhibited alarming symptoms while working out at the gym. The man suddenly experienced excessive sweating
   and what felt like gastric discomfort. Concerned, he was rushed to a specialized heart hospital,
    where doctors diagnosed him with a massive heart attack caused by a complete blockage of a major artery.
 However, what happened next nearly cost him his life.
